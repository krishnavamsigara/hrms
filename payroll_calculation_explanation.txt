3. CALENDAR DAYS DISSECTION & ATTENDANCE TALLY FORMULAS
----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Any payroll period consists of two fundamental classifications of calendar days:
  A. Scheduled Working Days (Office Work Days)
  B. Non-Working Days (Holidays & Company Weekly Offs / Weekends)

For the 31-day period (25-Aug-2026 to 24-Sep-2026):
  1. Total Holidays & Weekly Offs (H):
     - Total Holidays/Offs = 11 Days
     - Breakdown:
       * 2026-08-29 (Saturday Off)
       * 2026-08-30 (Sunday Off)
       * 2026-09-04 (Milad-un-Nabi / Festival Holiday)
       * 2026-09-05 (Saturday Off)
       * 2026-09-06 (Sunday Off)
       * 2026-09-12 (Saturday Off)
       * 2026-09-13 (Sunday Off)
       * 2026-09-14 (Vinayaka Chavithi / Festival Holiday)
       * 2026-09-19 (Saturday Off)
       * 2026-09-20 (Sunday Off)
       * 2026-09-24 (Special Company Holiday)

  2. Scheduled Office Working Days (W):
     Formula:
       Scheduled Working Days = Total Month Days - Total Holidays/Offs
       31 - 11 = 20 Days

  3. Attendance Realization during Working Days:
     Out of the 20 scheduled office working days:
       - Present Days (P): Days employee punched in / worked in office or approved WFH.
       - Paid Leaves (PL): Approved paid leaves (Casual / Sick / Earned leaves).
       - Absent / LOP Days (LOP): Unapproved absences or unpaid leave (Loss of Pay).

     Office Working Days Balance Formula:
       Office Working Days = Present Days + Paid Leaves + LOP Days
       20 = 18 + 0 + 2  (e.g., for BS00001)

  4. Total Paid Days Formula:
     Every employee is paid for their worked days PLUS all recognized holidays/offs PLUS paid leaves:
       Paid Days = Present Days + Holidays/Offs + Paid Leaves
       OR equivalently:
       Paid Days = Total Month Days - LOP Days
       31 - 2 = 29 Days

  5. COMPLETE ATTENDANCE TALLY CHECK:
     -------------------------------------------------------------------------
     TALLY EQUATION:
       Total Month Days (31) = Present (18) + Holidays/Offs (11) + LOP (2)
       31 = 18 + 11 + 2  ==> PERFECTLY BALANCED (100% TALLIED)
     -------------------------------------------------------------------------

4. SALARY & DEDUCTION MATHEMATICAL FORMULAS
----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
  1. Per-Day Salary Rate (Daily Wage):
     Standard monthly payroll computes daily salary based on total calendar days in the cycle period:
       Per-Day Rate = Salary Basis (Monthly Gross) / Total Month Days (31)

  2. Leave / LOP Deduction:
     Formula:
       Leave Deduction = (Salary Basis / Total Month Days) * LOP Days
     For BS00001:
       Salary Basis = ₹58,050.00
       Per-Day Rate = 58,050 / 31 = ₹1,872.5806...
       Leave Deduction = 1,872.5806 * 2 = ₹3,745.16

  3. Gross Earned Salary:
     Formula:
       Gross Salary = Salary Basis - Leave Deduction + Approved Additions
     For BS00001:
       Gross Salary = ₹58,050.00 - ₹3,745.16 = ₹54,304.84

  4. Statutory Deductions (D):
     Includes Provident Fund (PF Employee), Professional Tax (PT), ESI Employee, TDS:
     For BS00001:
       PF Employee = ₹1,800.00
       PT          = ₹0.00
       TDS         = ₹0.00
       Total Deductions = ₹1,800.00

  5. Net Take-Home Pay:
     Formula:
       Net Pay = Gross Earned Salary - Statutory Deductions
     For BS00001:
       Net Pay = ₹54,304.84 - ₹1,800.00 = ₹52,504.84
